A support chatbot is a conversational assistant that helps customers find information and resolve common questions through a chat interface. It can make support guidance easier to access, at any time, without requiring a customer to search through multiple pages.

## Common capabilities

Depending on how it is configured and what information it can access, a support chatbot may be able to:

- Answer frequently asked questions using approved help content
- Guide a customer through troubleshooting steps
- Ask follow-up questions to better understand a request
- Point to relevant articles, policies, or product instructions
- Hand a conversation over to a support team when a request needs personal attention

## Good support starts with good content

The chatbot's usefulness depends on the quality and coverage of its source information. Keep help articles accurate, clearly organized, and up to date. When an answer is uncertain or the question falls outside the available guidance, the chatbot should say so and offer an appropriate way to contact a person.

The exact behavior of a support chatbot depends on its implementation, connected knowledge sources, and handoff options. Review its answers regularly and update the underlying support content as products and policies change.
